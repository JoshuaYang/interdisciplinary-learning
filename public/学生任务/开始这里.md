# Student Mission Guide

This project drives the 3D visualization of the "Free-Radical Substitution of Methane" through molecular modeling and parameterized structural configuration.

Each group is responsible for one molecule or radical species. The template files are located in the `student_tasks/` directory. Use your chemical knowledge and experimental reference measurements to complete the configurations.

---

## Workflow

```
[1. Claim Molecule] ──> [2. Review Field Spec] ──> [3. Complete Config] ──> [4. Upload & Verify]
```

### 1. Molecule Assignments
- Group 1: `CH4.json` (Methane)
- Group 2: `CH3.json` (Methyl Radical)
- Group 3: `CH3Br.json` (Bromomethane)
- Group 4: `Br2.json` (Bromine)
- Group 5: `HBr.json` (Hydrogen Bromide)
- Group 6: `Br.json` (Bromine Radical)

---

### 2. Review Field Specifications
Read `field_spec.md` for required properties:
- `formula`: Chemical formula, e.g., `"CH4"` or `"CH3·"`
- `geometry`: Molecular geometry, e.g., `"regularTetrahedral"` or `"trigonalPlanar"`
- `hybridization`: Central atom hybridization, e.g., `"sp3"` or `"sp2"`
- `bondLengths`: Key bond lengths in Ångströms (Å)
- `bondAngles`: Key bond angles in degrees (°)
- `unpairedElectrons`: 1 for radicals, omit or 0 for closed-shell species

---

### 3. Verify in Workbench
Open the web application and import or visually construct your assigned molecule in Step 1. The validation system will automatically verify parameters against standard physical chemistry tolerances.
