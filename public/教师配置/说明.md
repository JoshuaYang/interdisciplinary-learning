# Teacher Guide & Curriculum Specifications

This project provides an interactive 3D instructional environment for senior secondary and undergraduate chemistry, focusing on the Free-Radical Substitution of Methane.

## File Organization

- `project.json`: Project metadata, units, data status, and file manifest.
- `catalog.json` (`分子参考.json`): Reference molecular geometries, bond lengths (NIST CCCBDB), and tolerances.
- `steps/01.json` - `steps/07.json`: Individual step animations, 3D instances, positions, rotations, and bond transformations.
- `students/` (`标准答案/`): Standard validated target molecular structures.

## 7 Pedagogical Stages
1. **Initial Species**: Observe methane tetrahedral structure and bromine Br—Br covalent bond.
2. **Photoexcitation**: Br₂ homolytic cleavage under UV light ($h\nu$).
3. **Substrate Approach**: Bromine radical approaches a C—H bond of methane.
4. **Collision Reaction ①**: C—H bond cleavage and H—Br bond formation; methyl radical generation.
5. **Intermediate Approach**: Methyl radical approaches another Br₂ molecule.
6. **Collision Reaction ②**: Methyl radical attacks Br₂, forming bromomethane ($\text{CH}_3\text{Br}$) and regenerating Br·.
7. **Net Summary**: Overview of net reaction stoichiometry and overall covalent bond balance.
